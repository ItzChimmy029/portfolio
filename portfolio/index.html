My process

Three connected stages. Feedback can send me back to any earlier one.

1

Research method

  • Understand goals and requirements
  • Identify the intended users
  • Research relevant references and approaches
  • Understand constraints and priorities
  • Define the expected outcome
I first understand the problem, the people experiencing it, and the outcome the project needs to achieve. This helps me make informed decisions before development begins.
2

Design and development workflow

  • Organize requirements
  • Plan structure and user flow
  • Design the interface
  • Develop the functionality
  • Make it responsive
  • Test, fix issues, improve usability
Once the requirements are clear, I plan the structure, design the experience, build the functionality, and test the result across devices to ensure it works as intended.
3

Feedback loop

  • Review a working version or prototype
  • Collect feedback where appropriate
  • Identify bugs and confusing areas
  • Make improvements
  • Test the changes and refine
I treat feedback as part of the development process. I review what works, identify what needs improvement, make changes, and test again until the solution better meets the project's needs.

Feedback returns to research, design or development, then the cycle runs again.
